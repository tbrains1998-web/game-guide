⌨ Shortcuts

    
These work while you're in the game.
      (Also listed when you right-click the gamepad icon by the clock.)

    

      
F10 holdAsk by voice
Say where you are and what you're stuck on, then let go. Beep = listening, lower beep = sent.

      
F7Next step
Answers come one short step at a time. Shows the next step of the latest answer, and reads it out when Read aloud is on.

      
F6Hide / show the overlay
The current step (and its circle) is shown over the game itself. F6 hides it until the next answer, or shows it again.

      
F4Where am I?
Takes a screenshot and answers in 3 lines: where you are, your objective right now, and what to do next. No spoilers or codes.

      
F8Read the screen aloud
Reads notes, letters, documents and subtitles on screen with the voice. Press again to stop.

      
F11Attach a screenshot
Sent along with your next question so the guide can see where you are. Press it again to attach more (up to 4), e.g. the clue first, then the puzzle.

      
F3 first run only (Unreal games)Sense discovery list
Press once in game, the first time you play with the Sense helper. It writes a list of the game's item types so Sense can learn which ones count. Not needed after that.

      
Esc in this windowStop
Stops an answer that's being written or read out.

❌ Didn't workThe answer failed in game: the guide first re-gives the same steps more precisely (you may have pressed something wrong) and asks for a screenshot to check, before trying a different answer. Asking the same question twice does the same.

Auto-detectSwitches game automatically when you play something else.

      
📍 Where am I?Same as F4, from the window (press F11 first if you want it to look at the screen).

      
Map: F4 with the map openPress F4 while the game map is on screen. The guide says where you are, where to go next, and draws the route on the map. Away from a checkpoint it uses your last map and tells you the nearest checkpoint to open it.

      
🗺 What's left here?A short list of what is still to do in the area you are in: done, probably not done, or unknown, with the undone ones circled on your latest map. Hint = rough place, Solution = exact spot.

      
Clues from F8When F8 reads a note, sign or dialogue, tasks it mentions (a place to go, a code, a name) are saved as "Clue" lines in Progress notes, and "Where do I go?" keeps them in mind.

      
⚔ Boss tipsA compact card for the boss you're facing: phases, attacks to dodge and their tells, what to use, the common mistake, the plan. Any question with "boss" or "how do I beat" in it gets this format too.

      
OverlayShows the current step over the game, with the circle drawn on the real button. Circles hide if you look away from where the screenshot was taken.

      
SenseAlways on, no key needed. When a pickup or collectible is within range (10 m by default), its spot pulses on screen; off screen, a big arrow at the edge, with the distance, points to it. The sound is off by default: use the "Sound: on/off" button to turn it on (then a soft sound repeats, faster when closer). Needs the small helper in the game folder (Install helper, with the game closed). It only reads the game, it never changes it.

      
Quest pathOn by default, no key needed. When the game has a target (the quest you track, or a door or lock you just opened and have not reached yet), a dotted glowing trail on the ground shows the way from your feet toward it, the next 30 m, with a small arrow at the end. The trail follows the paths you can really walk: along paths, up and down stairs and slopes, and around walls. Where the game has no walking paths for that spot, the dots sit on the ground in a straight line toward the target. Dots that would be drawn on top of your character are left out, near or far, so the trail looks like it goes behind you; the rest of the trail stays. When the target is behind you or off screen, an arrow at the edge shows the way and the metres left. "↑ 2 floors" means it is higher up; it only shows when the target is within 100 m (further away you just see the metres). It hides when you are within 5 m. Only main quests get a trail (side quests do not). When your main quest is in another area of the world, no trail is drawn: the Sense panel says "Main quest is in another area" with the area's name, and a short message pops up once. Turn it on or off with the "Quest path" button in the Sense panel. "Quest path follows the ground: off" makes the trail go straight again and stops asking the game for walking paths (use it if the game gets unstable; works at once, no game restart). Needs the Sense helper.
